CHAPTER 1 · ADR-008 & ADR-012 (HIGH-WATER MARK)

The 87/10/3 Transaction Profit Waterfall (TPWⓘ)

What This Means in Plain English

Whenever your account reaches a brand-new personal record high, 87 cents of every new profit dollar stays in your account to keep growing, 10 cents strengthens the shared family safety net (CFCP), and 3 cents pays for servers and data feeds (FAEP). If your account dips and climbs back up, you keep 100% of every recovery dollar—zero profit is shared until you pass your old high mark.

Under ADR-012 (Per-SCMA High-Water Mark)ⓘ, the 87/10/3 waterfall executes strictly on net new profit above each SCMA's prior High-Water Mark (HWM)—eliminating the 13% volatility tax during drawdown recovery. Profit earned while recovering below HWM stays 100% in the executing SCMA ($0.00 to CFCP and FAEP).

Rule A14 & ADR-012: 100% loss isolation within the executing SCMA, and 0% CFCP/FAEP skim during drawdown recovery below each SCMA's High-Water Mark (eliminating the volatility tax).

CHAPTER 3 · RULE A14 & RING-FENCED LEDGERS

Household Alpha — 100% SCMA Loss Isolation

What This Means in Plain English

Think of every family member's account as its own watertight room on a ship. If one relative's trade loses money, that loss stays 100% inside their own room—it can never drain a sibling's savings, a grandparent's account, or the shared family emergency pool.

Under Rule A14 (100% SCMA Loss Isolation), any realized trading loss remains 100% contained inside the executing SCMA. Sibling SCMAs, the CFCP Reserve Shield ⓘ, and FAEP never decrease to absorb an individual trading loss. Under ADR-012, subsequent winning trades by that SCMA remain 100% inside the SCMA until its equity recovers past its prior High-Water Mark—eliminating any volatility tax.

$25k Active Float Ceiling, IFAS Custody & Dynamic Outbox

The trading software only ever keeps up to $25,000 working on a venue so orders stay nimble. Everything you earn above $25,000 automatically sweeps out to safe bank custody managed by our separate bookkeeping system (IFAS).
