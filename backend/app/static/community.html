Pay-it-Forward (FSAP) Sponsorship Charter

A structured, ring-fenced way for financially stable relatives to lift another household out of high-interest debt and seed their future — with principal protection, variable realized pool participation, and zero shared trading risk.

1. Ring-Fenced Pool & Zero Shared Risk

Family sponsors (Patrons) contribute spare capital into the Familial Stability Advance Pool (FSAP), held separately inside our independent accounting system (IFAS). Every personal member account (SCMA) stays 100% walled off — helping a relative never pledges your savings or connects your account to anyone else's trading loss.

2. Principal Return & Variable Realized Participation

Sponsorship is neither a one-way handout nor a guaranteed-interest loan. Sponsors receive their full contributed principal back as advances are repaid, plus a transparent, time-weighted share of actual realized pool income (bank/Treasury interest on idle cash plus realized facility fees, minus expenses) — never paid from newer contributors' deposits.

3. Three-Stage Direct Relief & Automated Repayment

Approved advances (co-signed by the local Head of Household and Chief Administrator) flow in three stages: (1) direct payoff of high-interest creditors, (2) a starter seed in the relative's isolated PDEUE account, and (3) a modest household cash cushion. Trading runs untouched at 100%; repayment happens automatically only from excess cash sweeps (50% default, tunable 30%–70% by the Head of Household).

4. The Sovereign Choice & Revolving Multiplier

The moment a relative's advance reaches $0.00, all obligation ends completely. Only then are they invited — never required — to make The Sovereign Choice to sponsor the next cousin or grandchild. Recycled across five cycles, a single $100,000 family pool delivers $409,510 (at 90% recovery) to $452,438 (at 95% recovery) in cumulative family debt relief.

Transparent Charter Guardrails

  • Zero cross-collateralization: no Patron's personal account is ever pledged for another family member.
  • Dual-control verification: direct-to-creditor payoffs require both local Head of Household and Chief Administrator sign-off inside IFAS.
  • No guaranteed fixed interest and zero Ponzi cross-subsidy: sponsor participation comes strictly from actual realized pool earnings.
  • Phase I (Live Today): Family portals, Hall of Arms, Lineage Hearth, PDEUE University, and real-market observation run with zero bank links and zero live money at risk.
  • Phase II (Next Institutional Milestone): Activates the separate air-gapped IFAS accounting service, commercial trust bank rails, live profit sweeps, and live FSAP sponsorships.
