## 4 Chapter B fixtures and practice control

For ordinary adjustable teaching fixtures, the practice control is a labeled choice group offering 1.50%, 1.00%, and 0.50%. Initial accepted practice level is 1.50%. Proposed level remains separate until Apply practice reduction. The control accepts a strictly lower listed value; an equal selection produces “No change”; a higher selection produces “Higher authority required — no practice change.” No submitted value outside the set can become state. The choices demonstrate the accepted range without claiming the real slider's step size.

Do not present the 5.0% Tier-1 ceiling as a selectable Member value. It is always explanatory text. The broader 0.50%–2.00% range remains visible; the prototype's 1.50% initial state does not permit an increase to 2.00%. Reset restores 1.50% solely inside the ordinary adjustable learning example after a clear confirmation. BFROZEN and BCUSTODIAL reset to their own blocked fixture defaults; reset never clears their restriction. It does not teach an operational reset route.

No full-Kelly estimator, stake calculator, concentration model, liquidity model, or governance override is in scope.

Scenario selectors expose consequences; they are not switches that turn off real safeguards. In BHALT, disclose why practice submission is unavailable and route to the static governance explanation. The ordinary risk control cannot release this condition.” Explain that 0.00% is not a normal selectable slider value, restoration authority, permission to clear the freeze, or evidence that another safeguard passed. Keep the original 1.50% screenshot clearly labeled as the unchanged reference; do not edit its pixels to 0.00%. In this fixture, no risk choice or Apply practice reduction action is enabled.

BCUSTODIAL uses a text card: “Custodial SCMA — Member self-service risk adjustment unavailable. F2-H Head of Household authorization is required; all other applicable governance and immutable risk constraints still apply.” Do not invent a custodial percentage or approval button. No Member practice mutation is available. F2-H authorization is an explained route, not a simulated grant, automatic restoration or broader approval.

Selecting either blocked fixture discards any pending ordinary proposal, pauses media and displays its fixed facts. Repeated, stale or keyboard-triggered apply events cannot mutate it. Reset keeps the selected blocked fixture blocked. Selecting another named fictional fixture is labeled a change of learning example, never release of the frozen or custodial account. All explanations remain keyboard- and screen-reader-readable outside disabled controls. Practice remains text-only; it adds no audio script, timed caption or screenshot requirement.
