Your account and the Member workspace

## 1 Welcome to PDEUE

PDEUE, the Public Data Event Underwriting Engine, uses lawful public information to evaluate opportunities and prepare risk-bounded decisions. It is a Financial Generation Engine within the wider Financial Generation System, which governs identity, permissions, policy and records.

Your Member workspace helps you understand your own account, follow its capital context and ask educational questions. Automated analysis and candidate selection reduce the need to select individual market opportunities yourself. Analysis, underwriting and execution remain within their assigned authority.

> Your role: understand your account, use permitted requests and choose less risk when appropriate. The Member desktop is not a retail trading terminal. A chart or tutor answer does not authorize an order, transfer or increase in risk.

> Capital can lose value. Safeguards constrain activity but cannot guarantee a return or eliminate loss. Confirm whether your session is a demonstration or an authorized operating environment before relying on its values.

## 2 Your Self-Contained Member Account

Your Self-Contained Member Account, or SCMA, records your capital and activity separately from other Members. Figure M-02 shows the account boundary: another Member’s loss does not become your account’s liability. Your own account can still lose value.

![Three separately bounded SCMAs hold their own capital and losses. A separate CFCP reserve is not an individual Member account.](assets/M-02.png)

**Figure M-02 — Your account has its own boundary.** Concept diagram. Each SCMA remains separately partitioned; shared family structures do not make one Member responsible for another Member’s account loss.

| Account term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Seed principal | The initial capital recorded for your SCMA. |
| Available cash | Unreserved cash. Other controls can still restrict its use. |
| Reservation | Money set aside for a commitment so it cannot be used twice. It is not itself a loss. |
| Open position | An exposure that has not reached its final reconciled outcome. |
| Realized result | A gain or loss established through outcome and reconciliation records. |

Use only your own identity and account. If you hold another role, your Active Hat identifies the permission context for each action; roles do not combine automatically. Stop and report any wrong name or SCMA identifier before continuing.

## 3 The 87/10/3 Transaction Profit Waterfall

When eligible positive proceeds become realized and reconciled, the Transaction Profit Waterfall allocates them among three destinations. Figure M-03 shows the complete split and the different treatment of a loss.

![An eligible $100 realized positive amount allocates $87 to SCMA, $10 to CFCP and $3 to FAEP. Rounding residuals go to CFCP; losses stay in the executing SCMA.](assets/M-03.png)

**Figure M-03 — The 87/10/3 Transaction Profit Waterfall.** Illustrative example. The percentages apply to the declared eligible realized positive proceeds, not to the whole account balance. The three allocations conserve every cent.

### What stays yours and what serves shared purposes

The 87% share stays with the executing SCMA. The Central Familial Common Pool (CFCP) receives 10% for governed family resilience. The Founder/Chief Administrator Endowment Pool (FAEP) receives 3% for its dedicated administrative and endowment purpose. Shared allocations are not personal spending cash.

ADR-008 is the exact-cent accounting rule. Allocations must add back to the eligible whole, with any canonical rounding residual assigned to CFCP. Each event must identify its basis—for example, an eligible realized gain or yield. Do not apply the percentages to an arbitrary account total or an unrealized gain.

> Compounding means retained capital can contribute to future governed results. It does not promise growth, a fixed yield or unrestricted access to cash.

## 6 Capital Protection

Several safeguards apply together before capital can be committed. Figure M-05 shows how qualification, sizing and governance constrain the final permitted exposure. A percentage on its own never grants permission.

![Seven constraints jointly limit exposure: qualification, Quarter-Kelly, Tier-1 five percent ceiling, Member setting, concentration and liquidity, forty percent dry powder, and governance controls.](assets/M-05.png)

**Figure M-05 — The capital safety stack.** Concept diagram. Every applicable safeguard must pass. The 5.0% defensive ceiling is separate from the Member slider and is never a target that must be used.

Quarter-Kelly uses one quarter of the applicable full-Kelly sizing result as a ceiling input. It does not require that amount to be invested. The Tier-1 defensive per-opportunity ceiling is 5.0%; the Member’s operational setting is a separate, lower control.

The mandatory 40% dry-powder floor leaves at least 40% of applicable pre-dispatch total equity uncommitted after existing reservations and proposed commitments. Exact-cent calculation rounds the floor conservatively upward. Concentration, liquidity, quarantine or a halt can require still less exposure—or none.

## 7 Downward-Only Member Risk

The Downward-Only Risk Governor lets you choose less risk under Directive R-05. The Member operational range is 0.50%–2.00%, subject to your authorized current level and every other control. Figure M-06 shows the control and a reduction example.

![Actual risk control shows 1.50 percent. A separately labeled example arrow shows a reduction to 1.00 percent. Restoration requires separate governance.](assets/M-06.png)

**Figure M-06 — Choose a lower risk setting.** Actual demonstration UI with an explanatory example. The screenshot shows the existing setting; the diagram illustrates a possible reduction, not a change performed for this guide.

| Control | What it permits |
| --- | --- |
| Tier-1 5.0% ceiling | The system’s defensive per-opportunity upper bound. It is not a Member slider setting. |
| Member 0.50%–2.00% control | A reduction from the authorized current level. After reducing, you cannot independently restore a higher value here. |
| Higher-authority governance | A separate route for eligible restoration or expansion within applicable immutable limits. Permission is not automatic. |

To make an intended reduction, confirm your account and the current and proposed values, select “Apply reduction” once, then wait for the result and refreshed value. If it fails or reverts, stop and contact support. A lower preference does not cancel a filled position or reverse a settlement.

> A frozen or quarantined account can have a restriction beyond the normal slider range. Do not use the slider to interpret or release that restriction. Your first session does not require changing it.

## 8 Distributions and High-Watermark Handling

Active Float is capped at $25,000 per SCMA. Eligible settled excess follows a governed high-watermark sweep rather than expanding venue float. Figure M-07 shows the relationship to IFAS, the Independent Financial Accounting and Authoritative-Record System.

![Seven-step governed flow from the SCMA and $25,000 per-SCMA high-watermark, through the signed outbox and IFAS, to external custody processing and a reconciled wealth record.](assets/M-07.png)

**Figure M-07 — High watermark and IFAS capital flow.** Process diagram. Staging or sending an accounting event does not prove external payment completion. IFAS acknowledgement and external settlement are distinct stages.

IFAS independently records and reconciles financial-generation events and external banking records. Total wealth can remain above the operational float cap and continue its separately governed off-venue path. Neither the cap nor a sweep guarantees growth.

The $25,000 Active Float high-watermark does not cap total Member wealth; eligible capital can continue in the governed off-venue compounding architecture associated with IFAS, subject to the applicable custody, yield, reconciliation, and status records.

> Read the stage precisely: scheduled, staged, sent, acknowledged and externally settled are different states. A signed accounting event or IFAS receipt alone does not prove that your bank received funds.

### The Green/Yellow/Red Distribution Gateway

A distribution request follows a governed path based on its amount and impact. Figure M-08 explains the three tiers. Use your approved household request process; the illustrated desktop does not show a distribution-request button.

![Green: positive requests up to $100. Yellow: above $100 through $500, with F1 notice. Red: above $500, with twenty-four-hour cooling-off and F2-H co-signature. All lanes remain governed.](assets/M-08.png)

**Figure M-08 — The three tier distribution gateway.** Process diagram. Dollar bands classify a positive request. They do not promise immediate payment or remove other applicable controls. Waiting 24 hours alone does not approve a Red request.

Green covers positive requests up to and including $100.00. Yellow covers $100.01–$500.00 and adds impact awareness and notice to your assigned F1 Peer Guide. Red covers amounts above $500.00 and requires a 24-hour cooling-off period plus the authorized F2-H Head of Household co-signature and any other required governance.

Before submitting, confirm the purpose, exact amount, account and permitted destination through the approved process. Then follow the request’s status. F1 notice is not a co-signature; F2-H co-signature does not bypass another required approval. A waiting period ending does not itself authorize execution.

> Do not assume a request classification, notification or approval is a completed payment. Confirm external settlement separately. Never place banking credentials in the tutor or a Member prompt.

## 9 AI Literacy Tutor and Human Support

Use the AI Literacy Tutor for explanations such as “How does the 87/10/3 waterfall work?” or “Why can I lower but not raise my risk setting?” Ask one general question at a time. For a personal balance, permission or unresolved status, use the authorized human support route in Figure M-09.

![Four support routes distinguish tutor education, F1 guidance, F2-H household co-signature and higher-governance escalation. Advice and recommendations do not grant execution authority.](assets/M-09.png)

**Figure M-09 — Find the right support and authority.** Authority guide. Start with the person or tool whose role fits the question. Education, recommendation, co-signature and execution are different permissions.

### Keep education separate from authority

The tutor cannot approve an order, increase a ceiling, move capital, reveal another account or override a halt. Advice and recommendations are not execution authority. A family observer may help you read the screen but does not acquire co-signature rights.

F1 Peer Guides provide education and guidance. F2-H Heads of Household perform assigned household governance, including permitted domestic co-signatures. Higher governance or specialist review handles matters outside that scope. Technical support can help with a loading or session problem; technical access does not grant financial authority.

> Keep passwords, access tokens, venue keys, banking details, private medical information and other Members’ data out of tutor questions. Use the approved private channel for account-specific identifiers.

## 10 Statuses, Problems, and Common Questions

Read status before relying on a value or repeating an action. Figure M-10 explains the supported safety vocabulary. “Available” describes a usable value or permitted state in context; it is not blanket permission to spend.

![Eight status meanings and actions cover normal, pending, qualification pending, stale or unavailable, quarantined, halted, pending settlement, and higher authority required.](assets/M-10.png)

**Figure M-10 — Read status before acting.** Status guide, not a screenshot of badges. Wording can vary by view. A missing badge does not establish that data is current, approved or settled.

Redacted means your current role is not permitted to see the value. Do not try to reconstruct it. For a wrong account, unexpected balance or rejected risk change, stop and record the displayed facts. Do not correct a balance yourself or bypass a rejection through another route.

For support, record the time and timezone, account reference, status text, expected result and actual result. Include a request or settlement identifier if one is available, using an approved private channel. Refresh a stuck page once; if the problem remains, ask for help rather than repeating a financial action.

## 12 Core Member Rules

- Use only your own account and the permissions of your Active Hat.

- Keep illustrations, unrealized gains and pending results separate from reconciled account evidence.

- Read exact amounts and statuses. A sweep label is not a bank receipt, and a yield label is not earned income.

- Reduce risk only through the permitted control. Do not bypass ceilings, rejection, quarantine or a halt.

- Keep CFCP and FAEP distinct from personal spending. Follow the governed distribution path.

- Protect private information. Ask the appropriate human when identity, authority or financial status is unclear.
